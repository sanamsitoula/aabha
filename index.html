The room

Twelve tables. One hearth. No shortcuts.

Aabha seats twelve tables a night in a single room built around a wood-fed hearth. Everything that reaches the table — the bread, the vegetables, the meat — passes over that fire.

Menus are short and change with what the valley's growers bring us that week. We hold every reservation for fifteen minutes past its time, then release it, so book the slot you mean to keep.
